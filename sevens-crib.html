Sevens Crib

Two-hand cribbage, dealt big. 7 cards each, 1 straight to the crib, then everyone throws 2. Race to 121.

The twist: instead of dealing 6 and throwing 2, you're dealt 7 cards and 1 goes face-down to the crib immediately. Each player then throws 2 more — so hands and the crib both land at 5 cards + the cut. Bigger hands, bigger scores, faster games.
